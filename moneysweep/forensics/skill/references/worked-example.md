# Worked Example — running the method end-to-end

A held-out test. Read this to see the chain applied, including where it produces a **null** result.

---

## CASE A: Whitefish Energy / PREPA (2017) — the machine, running

### The chain

| Step | Finding | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **PROJECT** | Restore Puerto Rico's electrical grid after Hurricane María. | T1 |
| **PRIME** | **Whitefish Energy Holdings.** Contract up to **$300M**, signed **26 Sept 2017 — six days after landfall.** No bid. | T1 |
| **SUBS** | **Whitefish had NO CREWS.** It subcontracted everything: $319/hr per subcontracted lineman, $15,000 per S61 helicopter. | T1 |
| **FINANCIALS** | **Two employees.** Two years old. No catastrophe experience. Solicited the contract **via LinkedIn**. | T1 |
| **OUTCOME** | Cancelled 31 Oct 2017. Billed **$144.2M**. FEMA ruled **$32.9M ineligible**. **Collected $96.3M** via bankruptcy settlement. **No charges filed against anyone.** | T1 |

### The tests

- **Capacity: FAIL, catastrophically.** 2 employees / $300M contract.
- **Pass-through: ~100%.** The company had no crews. It was a billing layer. **This is the finding.**

### Machine score: 6/6
Emergency (real) · no-bid · capacity gap · total pass-through · geographic connection to a federal official *(T3 — Zinke denied involvement; do NOT assert causation)* · FEMA had "significant questions" about the award.

### WHY — ranked
1. **Pass-through/conduit (T1).** A firm with no crews cannot restore a grid. It sold access, not labor.
2. **Emergency exception abuse (T1).** No-bid, six days post-landfall.
3. **Federal reimbursement as the weak payer (T1).** FEMA OIG found PREPA overpaid Whitefish + Cobra by **~$2 billion**, in violation of federal cost principles, with supervision failure assigned to **both PREPA and FEMA**.

**Note the enforcement asymmetry:** the companion contract (Cobra, $1.29B) *did* produce arrests — of a **FEMA official** and the contractor's president. The bribery happened on the **federal reimbursement side**. That is where to look in disaster-funded work.

## CASE B: Carraízo Dredging / PRASA (2023–) — the null result

**This is the more important example.** The investigator expected the machine. It isn't there. Reporting that honestly is the job.

### The chain

| Step | Finding | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **PROJECT** | Dredge Carraízo Reservoir. Announced ~**$70M** (2022). **Contracted $93,149,800** (Jan 2023) — a **33% gap nobody reported.** | T1 |
| **PRIME** | **Great Lakes Dredge & Dock Co., LLC.** Runs to **30 June 2028**. | T1 |
| **SUBS** | **6.2% subaward ratio — and both records are GLDD subcontracting to its OWN subsidiary.** Zero external pass-through. | T1 |
| **FINANCIALS** | **$198.5M federal awards / 78 transactions.** Charleston Post 45 ($304M), Miami, Jacksonville, Savannah, Boston. Publicly traded. "Other than small business." | T1 |
| **OUTCOME** | Work ongoing. **$51.4M disbursement gap. 24 months of missing monitoring records.** | T1 |

### The tests

- **Capacity: PASS.** GLDD owns dredges and has decades of USACE work.
- **Pass-through: PASS (6.2%, self-subcontracted).**

**The prime is sound. Say so.**

### So why the gap?

**The oversight-vacuum check (§6) is what cracked it:**

- Dredging contract: **31 Jan 2023 → 30 June 2028**
- CEI/inspection contract (**CSA-Louis Berger JV LLC**, $10,386,954): **22 Jul 2021 → 30 June 2023 — EXPIRED**

**The inspector's contract covered the first five months of a five-and-a-half-year job.**

The "24 months of missing monitoring records" are not missing. **They were never generated, because there was no inspector.**

### WHY — ranked
1. **Oversight vacuum (T1 on the contract dates; T4 on causation).** ← the answer
2. **Federal cost-reimbursement exposure (T2).** The CEI's parent, **Louis Berger Group**, paid **$69.3M** in penalties and its chairman pled guilty to a **20-year conspiracy inflating overhead on cost-reimbursable federal contracts** — the exact billing structure at Carraízo. **It was never debarred.**
3. **Corruption at the prime — REJECTED (T0).** Tested and not found.

### The lesson

The investigator arrived expecting Whitefish and found something quieter and more plausible: **a real contractor doing real work with nobody watching.** Had the method been used to confirm a prior belief rather than test it, the actual mechanism would have been missed entirely.

**A skill that only ever finds guilt is not an instrument. It's a mirror.**
