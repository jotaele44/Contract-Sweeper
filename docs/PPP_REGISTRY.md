# Puerto Rico PPP registry

Every public-private partnership concession moneysweep-pr tracks, where its data
comes from, and how far it has got. The machine-readable companion is
`reports/ppp_coverage.json`, regenerated by `scripts/build_ppp_coverage.py` from
committed tables — treat that file as the counts of record and this document as
the reasoning behind them.

## Why this exists

"Do we have all the PPPs?" had no answerable form. PPP facts were spread across
four places that never referenced each other:

- `data/canonical_v1/projects.csv` — the canonical project table.
- `data/reference/pr_p3_concessions.csv` — promoted P3 concessions. The raw
  scrape lands in gitignored `data/staging/processed/pr_p3_contracts.csv`, which
  was **read by nothing** until this registry landed; `--promote` copies it here,
  and only this committed file feeds the canonical table.
- the ACT/ACUDEN transition workbook under `data/raw/act_transition/` — real
  concession contracts nobody had extracted.
- `registries/source_registry.yaml` — the acquisition surfaces.

A concession could be "in the repo" in one sense and absent in every sense that
mattered. This registry names the concessions first and asks what the repo holds
about each, so a gap shows up as a gap.

## Spatial extent, and why most PPPs have no location

`spatial_extent` records how much of Puerto Rico a concession physically covers:

| Value | Meaning | Geometry a spatial producer can resolve |
|---|---|---|
| `site` | one physical location | a point |
| `corridor` | a route across municipalities | a LineString |
| `islandwide` | a territory-wide system | none — no single point exists |
| `unknown` | not yet determined | none, pending review |

This is the field that keeps the location chain honest. Four of the six
concessions are island-wide systems: LUMA does not operate a grid *in San Juan*,
it operates the grid. Their `municipality_id` in `projects.csv` records the
administrative seat of the lead agency, which is a real fact about the agency and
a false one about the asset.

`moneysweep/federation/canonical_v1_bridge.py` therefore federates a location
only for `site` rows. Shipping San Juan for LUMA would produce confident,
wrong correlations against everything else in San Juan — worse than shipping
nothing. This is the same distortion
`scripts/run_contract_finance_geo_reasoning.py` measures in its
`san_juan_hq_bias_report`.

The practical consequence: **one concession currently federates a location**
(LMM Airport → Carolina). That is not a data-quality failure; it is what the
concession portfolio actually looks like.

### On the P3 portal scraper

`_scrape_p3_portal` reads anchor text and hrefs only. It previously also emitted
`contract_value` and `sector` by sweeping the whole page for `$N` patterns and
sector words and indexing them by link position, which attached the i-th dollar
figure on the page to the i-th project. Those fields are now left empty for
portal rows; per-project values need a detail-page fetch, not a page sweep.
Expect the portal to contribute names and URLs, not amounts.

## Boundary with aguayluz-pr

Three of these concessions — LUMA, Genera, and the PRASA O&M agreement — operate
water and power systems, and `thehub-pr`'s producer map assigns water,
wastewater, power, and outage records to
[`aguayluz-pr`](https://github.com/jotaele44/aguayluz-pr).

The split this registry assumes:

- **moneysweep-pr owns the concession** — the agreement, its term, its value, the
  operator entity, and the contracts under it. That is a public-money fact.
- **aguayluz-pr owns the operational record** — outages, service quality, rates,
  and recovery work performed under the concession.

So "LUMA holds a 15-year T&D concession worth $2B" is a moneysweep row, and "the
grid went down in Ponce on Tuesday" is an aguayluz row. The hub correlates them
rather than either producer duplicating the other.

This is recorded here as the assumption in force, not as an agreement — it has
not been confirmed with aguayluz-pr, and that confirmation is a follow-up.

## Regenerating

```bash
python3 scripts/download_p3.py --force --promote  # refresh + promote the surface
python3 scripts/parse_act_transition_ppp.py     # concession contracts from the workbook
python3 scripts/ingest_projects.py              # promote to canonical_v1
python3 scripts/build_edges.py                  # LOCATED_IN edges follow projects
python3 scripts/build_ppp_coverage.py           # regenerate reports/ppp_coverage.json
python3 scripts/federation_export.py --mode test --now <fixed-timestamp>
```
